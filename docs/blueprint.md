# **App Name**: TradeFlow

## Core Features:

- Inventory Dashboard: Real-time view of product quantities, statuses (available, incoming, sold), and warehouse locations.
- Automated Stock Updates: Automatic stock level adjustments triggered by product arrivals, sales, and manual corrections.
- Sales Recording: Log sales with client details, products, quantities, prices, payment type (cash/partial/loan), and sales date.
- Client Loan Management: Track client loans, amounts paid, due dates, and remaining balances.
- Supplier Payment Tracking: Manage supplier payments, total payable amounts, amounts paid, and remaining balances, linking incoming products to supplier invoices.
- Financial Overview: Dashboard providing total inventory value, receivables, payables, and sales figures. Provides cash flow and insights and the LLM will act as a tool for presenting different analyses, such as projections or estimates based on existing records.
- User Notifications: Receive stock alerts or inform customers regarding expiring debt using SMS/Telegram/Whatsapp notifications.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Navy blue (#2E5266) to convey trust and stability.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F4F7), a desaturated version of the primary color to maintain a professional, yet neutral backdrop.
- Accent color: Teal (#39A6A3) to highlight key actions and data points, creating visual interest without overwhelming the user.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a sans-serif font known for its clean and readable design, ensuring clear presentation of business data.
- Use simple, monochrome icons to represent different inventory statuses, transaction types, and financial metrics.
- Employ a clear, grid-based layout to organize information effectively across both desktop and mobile platforms, focusing on key performance indicators at the top.
- Incorporate subtle transitions and animations for loading states and data updates, enhancing the user experience without causing distractions.
